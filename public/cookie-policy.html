Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how Venoms Index uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences, improve performance, and provide a better user experience.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Understand how users interact with our site (analytics)
  • Improve performance and reliability

We do not use cookies to collect personal information for advertising or resell your data.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Needed for the website to function (e.g., page navigation).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Preference Cookies: Store your preferences (e.g., language or theme).

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the website.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools (like analytics providers) that place cookies on your device. These providers have their own privacy and cookie policies.

6. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Please check this page for the latest version.
